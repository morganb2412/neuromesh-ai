# Prespecified hypotheses

- H1: Evidence-linked retrieval improves citation support over no retrieval on source-dependent questions.
- H2: Temporal withdrawal reduces answers based on superseded metadata after a source correction.
- H3: Episodic context improves delayed recall of operator interactions when those interactions are relevant and permitted.
- H4: Graph paths improve multi-hop evidence attribution over title-only retrieval, at a measurable latency and token cost.

The null for each hypothesis is no improvement under matched task and model budgets. The currently drafted pilot can only probe H1 after manual task review and an authorized provider run. It cannot evaluate H2–H4.
